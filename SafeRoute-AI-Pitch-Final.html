Four steps from headline to safe home.

Collect

Pull local news, police bulletins, open government data — and community reports & reviews from women using the app. Every hour, in English and Kannada.

→ raw articles + reports

Understand

An LLM extracts what happened, where, when and how severe — then geocodes and de-duplicates across sources.

→ geo-tagged incidents
03

Score

Every road segment gets a live risk score that fades as incidents age and shifts with the time of day.

→ city risk graph

16 — Challenges & responsibility

  
We’ve thought about what could go wrong.

  

    
Risk
Reporting bias
News over-covers some areas and under-reports others; many crimes never reach the press.
Mitigation
Blend news with official stats & community reports; show confidence levels; never claim “zero risk”.

    
Risk
Stigmatising neighbourhoods
Labelling whole localities “unsafe” can harm residents and businesses.
Mitigation
Score road segments & time windows, not communities. No “dangerous area” labels; data decays over time.

    
Risk
Misinformation & duplicates
One event reported by 5 outlets — or a rumour — can inflate risk.
Mitigation
Embedding-based de-duplication, cross-source verification, and human review for high-severity items.

    
Risk
Privacy
A safety app handles sensitive location data of vulnerable users.
Mitigation
Location processed on-device; shared only during SOS / Guardian mode; anonymous reports; DPDP Act compliant.

  

  

    
79%vs61%
of Indians would share their location so police can find them in an emergency — vs the global average
Unisys Security Index 2020

    
Why this works in India
Here, safety comes before privacy.
In many Western markets street safety isn’t a daily worry, so the debate is mostly about data privacy. In India, getting home safely is an everyday concern for women — so people are far more willing to share location when it protects them. That makes features like live SOS tracking, police hand-off and community reports far more valuable — and more widely adopted — here. We honour that trust: sharing is opt-in, SOS-only, and DPDP-compliant.
